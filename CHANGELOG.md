# Changelog

## [1.3.0] - 2026-11-03
### Added
- **Morph spells:** Rowan the Mage sells nine spells that turn you into a creature. Water Rat, Giant Gnat, Famished Ripper, Bloodfly, Ripper, Boar, Shadowripper, Harpy and Bear unlock from level 1 to level 20. Learning one needs a runestone, and casting it costs health.
- A creature form grows with you. Mind makes it last longer and hit harder, Strength gives it more health, Dexterity lets it crit, and every level past the spell's level adds damage.
- In creature form the weapon swap key switches the creature's attacks and the dodge key uses its special attack.
- New talents at Rowan: Beast Bond I to III, Feral Recovery, Predator's Feast, Lingering Form and Second Skin.
- An "End morph" key under MageDrova in the options turns you back early. It is H by default and can be rebound.
- Creature forms work in co-op with the Drova Coop mod.

### Changed
- The options page is in English and German only. The French text is gone.

### Fixed
- Rowan's talents no longer cover the attribute panel in his learn window. Talents that do not fit go into a second row.
- Talent tooltips no longer show their description twice.

Needs Drova Modding API 0.8.0.

## [1.2.0] - 2026-10-11
- Co-op support: each player keeps their own mage path when playing with the Drova Coop mod.
